# Birth to Death (B2D) - Imagine Cup Edition 🏆

**Gamify Your Life. Master Your Focus.**

B2D is an Android application designed to help individuals with ADHD manage tasks, improve focus, and build healthy habits through gamification and Augmented Reality (AR).

## 🚀 Features

### 1. ⚔️ Gamified "Adventure Log"
Turn boring to-do lists into epic quests.
- **XP & LevelingSystem**: Earn Experience Points (XP) for every task you complete. Level up to unlock rewards.
- **Streak Tracking**: Maintain a daily streak (🔥) to build consistency.
- **Swipe Gestures**: Satisfying swipe-to-complete (Left -> Right) actions for instant dopamine hits.

### 2. 🐉 Interactive AR Companion
A virtual pet that lives in your world.
- **Reactive AI**: The 3D model reacts to your touch and productivity.
- **Engagement**: Tap the companion to see it scale and animate!

### 3. ⏱️ Focus Timer (Pomodoro)
 combat time blindness with visual queues.
- **Visual Countdown**: A clear, large timer to help you stay on track.
- **Rewards**: Earn bonus XP for completing focus sessions without interruption.

### 4. 🧘 Yoga AI Instructor
Practice mindfulness with a virtual guide.
- **Guided Sessions**: Follow a sequence of poses (Breathing, Tree Pose, Warrior).
- **AI Feedback**: Receive text-based encouragement and form correction tips.
- **Mindfulness**: Earn massive XP rewards for completing daily wellness sessions.

### 5. 🛍️ Rewards Shop
Spend your hard-earned XP.
- **Customization**: Buy new auras, skins, and environments for your AR companion.
- **Motivation**: Tangible virtual rewards for real-world productivity.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- **AR Engine**: Sceneview (ARCore)
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Build System**: Gradle (Kotlin DSL)

## 📱 How to Run

1.  **Clone the Repository**:
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/kushagrakumar/b2d-production.git
    ```
2.  **Open in Android Studio**:
    - Select the `android-app` folder.
3.  **Build & Run**:
    - Connect an Android device (with ARCore support) or use an Emulator.
    - Run the `app` configuration.

## 🧠 Why ADHD Focus?

People with ADHD often struggle with executive dysfunction, time blindness, and lack of motivation for clear tasks. B2D addresses this by:
- **Externalizing Motivation**: Immediate XP rewards replace delayed gratification.
- **Visualizing Time**: The Focus Timer makes time passage concrete.
- **Body Doubling**: The AR Companion and Yoga AI provide a sense of presence and accountability.
